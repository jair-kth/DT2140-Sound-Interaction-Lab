    • Torpedo launch on upward tilt

    When pointing the phone straight upward, you hear the sound of firing a torpedo. We imagined a game scenario where the player aims and fires in a specific direction, using the upward orientation as a clear, intentional launch gesture. Triggering once per gesture makes the shot feel distinct and prevents accidental repeated firing while holding the phone in position.

    Developed by Maurizio Berta and Laura McHugh
    for DT2140 Multimodal Interfaces Sound Interaction Lab
